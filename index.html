How progress works

XP: 10 points for a correct answer or “Got It,” once per word per day.

Mastered: three successful recalls on separate days. A missed answer starts that word’s mastery count again.

Review: words return after 1, 3, 7, 14, then 30 days. Missed words return tomorrow. Existing mastered words keep their status and are ready for review.
